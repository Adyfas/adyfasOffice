Warung Go was built as an innovative solution to support Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Indonesia. The core concept is to connect local merchants with their surrounding communities through smart location tracking and digital ordering.

<Section/>

The platform orchestrates a three-sided ecosystem with distinct roles:
- **Customers**: Can discover nearby stores, place orders, and track deliveries.
- **MSME Merchants**: Get a platform to manage their products, showcase storefronts, and receive digital orders.
- **Delivery Drivers**: Couriers who handle the logistics of delivering orders from merchants to customers.

<Section/>

Upon opening the application, users are greeted with an interactive map powered by Leaflet and CARTO Analytics. The app instantly detects the user's location and recommends the top 10 closest MSME storefronts, encouraging local discovery and physical visits.

<Section/>

In simple terms, Warung Go acts as a localized, community-centric delivery network—combining the strengths of a map finder and an e-commerce platform to empower neighborhood vendors. 🚀
