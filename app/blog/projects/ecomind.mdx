This project was developed for a national web design competition, and the experience helped me grow significantly as a developer.

<Section/>

Themed around "Creative Ideas," I chose to address environmental awareness—an area with huge potential for education in Indonesia. Ecomind Green Era features a custom machine learning model that classifies organic and inorganic waste. The website identifies the waste category, estimates its decomposition time, and explains the environmental impact of leaving it untreated. This explanation is powered by the custom Ecomind AI assistant.

<Section/>

To build this, I chose Next.js and Tailwind CSS. Since running local machine learning models was too heavy for my laptop setup, I integrated the AI features using cloud APIs, ensuring a fast and responsive user experience.

<Section/>

The project advanced to the final round, leading to a live presentation and interview with the judges. They were pleasantly surprised by a vocational school student tackling complex integrations like AI classification: *"How can a vocational school student learn this much?"*

<Section/>

While the judges pointed out some valuable areas for improvement, they were highly impressed with the implementation. During the awards announcement, Ecomind secured **2nd Place** 🥈!
