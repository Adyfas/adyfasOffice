This is the next competition I'm participating in. I've learned a lot here and learned a lot about myself and my future as a Full Stack Web Developer.
<Section/>
The theme of this competition was "Creative Ideas," so I chose environmental education because Indonesia has very little in the way of that. I also added a few features, such as organic and non-organic waste detection, so I can identify what kind of waste it is, how long it takes to decompose, and what the consequences are if left untreated. All of this will be explained by the Ecomind AI on the website.

<Section/>
I built the website using NextJS technology and TailwindCSS as the style. I created the AI ​​myself, but since my laptop can't handle that much AI data, I used APIs for both AIs.

<Section/>
After the website was finished, I entered the final round, where I was interviewed by the judges. They asked me a lot of questions. The judges were quite surprised, "How can a vocational school student learn this much?" I thought for a moment, "I don't think it's that far off," because it was all basic.

<Section/>
The judges were quite impressed with my work, but there were also shortcomings with the website. After the completion, they asked me questions about the announcement of the winners. That's when my name was called, and I won second place in the web competition.
