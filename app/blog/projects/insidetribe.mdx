Inside Tribe is the best website I've ever created (in my opinion). From there, I also improved my front-end skills considerably. I entered the Difest Web Design competition. I was given a theme: creativity, and yes, I chose "closed culture in Indonesia," which is why I named this website Inside Tribe.

<Section/>
The website explains a wealth of Indonesian culture that hasn't been exposed to the media. I explain it in detail, from its origins, traditional houses, traditional clothing, and so on, so that we, as Indonesians, can be proud of our diverse culture and unique customs.

<Section/>
On the website, I combined everything, from a clean layout to cool animations for each element. I'd say there's no other Indonesian website with a modern appearance like this one, haha ​​(just kidding).
<Section/>


After the website was finished and I made it to the final round, I secured second place, but I'm okay with that because I'm quite proud of my own creation.
